# Concrete elliptic-curve instances

These theories specialize the short-Weierstrass group library to secp256k1,
P-256, BN254 and BLS12-381. They separate curve parameters and standard-generator
order certificates from full rational-point cardinalities, and provide twist
embeddings into degree-12 extension fields. See the [project guide](../../README.md)
for the surrounding field, group and elliptic-curve layers.

The source theorem statements are authoritative for hypotheses and attributes.

## Prime-field curves

* [secp256k1Instance](prime_field/secp256k1InstanceScript.sml):
  `y^2 = x^3 + 7`, the standard generator, the full `secp256k1_group`, and
  `secp256k1_subgroup` generated by that point. Start with `secp256k1_curve`,
  `secp256k1_generator_order` and `secp256k1_subgroup_card`.
* [p256Instance](prime_field/p256InstanceScript.sml):
  P-256 with `a = p256_p - 3`, its standard generator and generated subgroup.
  Entry points are `p256_curve`, `p256_generator_order` and
  `p256_subgroup_card`.
* [bn254G1Instance](prime_field/bn254G1InstanceScript.sml):
  `y^2 = x^3 + 3` over the BN254 base field, with generator `(1,2)`.
  Entry points are `bn254_g1_curve`, `bn254_g1_generator_order` and
  `bn254_g1_subgroup_card`. This is G1, not the quadratic-field G2 twist.
* [bls12381G1Instance](prime_field/bls12381G1InstanceScript.sml):
  `y^2 = x^3 + 4` over the BLS12-381 base field and its standard prime-order
  subgroup. Entry points are `bls12381_g1_curve`,
  `bls12381_g1_generator_order` and `bls12381_g1_subgroup_card`.
* [cofactorOneInstances](prime_field/cofactorOneInstancesScript.sml):
  full point counts for secp256k1, P-256 and BN254 G1. The public families
  `*_cubic_root_free` and `*_no_two_torsion` support `secp256k1_card`,
  `p256_card` and `bn254_g1_card`. The corresponding `*_cofactor` theorems
  express cofactor one, and `*_generated_full_group` identify each standard
  generated subgroup with its full curve group.
* [bls12381Cardinality](prime_field/bls12381CardinalityScript.sml):
  the full BLS12-381 G1 count. `bls12381_full_certificate_subgroup_def` combines
  two generated subgroups; `bls12381_full_certificate_subgroup_product_card`
  and `bls12381_full_certificate_subgroup_card` supply the cardinality divisor.
  Main results are `bls12381_g1_full_cardinality`,
  `bls12381_g1_full_cofactor` and `bls12381_g1_prime_subgroup_not_full`.
* [secp256k1Endomorphism](prime_field/secp256k1EndomorphismScript.sml):
  the cube-root map `secp256k1_endo`, its base-field constant
  `secp256k1_beta` and scalar eigenvalue `secp256k1_lambda`.
  `secp256k1_endo_group` and `secp256k1_endo_scalar` give the structural
  interface; `secp256k1_endo_generator` and
  `secp256k1_endo_is_lambda_scalar` identify the map with scalar
  multiplication, the latter on all curve points using cofactor one.

The four instance theories also expose `*_generator_member`,
`*_finite_group`, `*_generator_annihilated`, `*_subgroup_is_subgroup` and
`*_subgroup_cyclic`, using their respective curve prefixes above.

## Quadratic-field twists and cardinalities

* [pairingTwistInstances](pairing/pairingTwistInstancesScript.sml):
  the BN254 twist with `b = 3/(9+i)` and the BLS12-381 twist with `b = 4+4*i`.
  For each prefix `bn254_g2` and `bls12381_g2`, definitions `*_a_def`,
  `*_b_def`, `*_generator_def`, `*_group_def` and `*_subgroup_def` fix the
  curve and standard generated subgroup. Use `*_curve`, `*_generator_member`,
  `*_finite_group`, `*_generator_annihilated`, `*_generator_order`,
  `*_subgroup_is_subgroup`, `*_subgroup_cyclic` and `*_subgroup_card`.
  `bn254_g2_twist_coefficient` checks the normalized coefficient against
  `3/(9+i)`. This theory gives standard-generator orders, not full point counts.
* [bn254TwistCardinality](pairing/bn254TwistCardinalityScript.sml):
  defines `bn254_g2_full_cofactor = 2 * bn254_p - bn254_n`, the full order,
  and a separate full-order certificate point `bn254_g2_full_generator`.
  Use `bn254_g2_full_generator_order`, `bn254_g2_cardinality`,
  `bn254_g2_group_cardinality`, `bn254_g2_full_cofactor_relation` and
  `bn254_g2_prime_subgroup_is_proper`. The clearing interface is
  `bn254_g2_cofactor_clearing`; `bn254_g2_full_generator_cofactor_order`
  gives the order of the cleared full-order point.
* [bls12381TwistCardinalityCertificates](pairing/bls12381TwistCardinalityCertificatesScript.sml):
  definitions and long arithmetic checks for the BLS12-381 full G2 count.
  `bls12381_g2_full_cofactor_factorization` relates the full count to a large
  order times the second factor, `bls12381_g2_torsion_factor = 299`.
  Main certificate interfaces are `bls12381_g2_large_point_scalar_tests`,
  `bls12381_g2_cm_endomorphism`, `bls12381_g2_cm_third`,
  `bls12381_g2_cm_scalar_exclusions` and
  `bls12381_g2_cubic_nonresidue_certificate`.
* [bls12381TwistCardinality](pairing/bls12381TwistCardinalityScript.sml):
  the cardinality argument using those certificates. Start with
  `bls12381_g2_large_point_order`, `bls12381_g2_second_point_order`,
  `bls12381_g2_cardinality_subgroup_card`, `bls12381_g2_full_cardinality`,
  `bls12381_g2_group_card`, `bls12381_g2_subgroup_is_proper` and
  `bls12381_g2_full_cofactor_clearing`.
* [bls12381TwistPrimeData.sml](pairing/bls12381TwistPrimeData.sml) and its
  [signature](pairing/bls12381TwistPrimeData.sig): untrusted Lucas witness
  data, exposed as `certificates` for `PRIME_CERT_CONV` in the BLS12-381
  twist cardinality theory. This module does not itself export a primality theorem.

## Twist embeddings

* [pairingTwistMorphisms](pairing/pairingTwistMorphismsScript.sml):
  defines `bn254_twist_embedding` and `bls12381_twist_embedding` into
  `bn254_fp12_curve_group` and `bls12381_fp12_curve_group`. Field inclusion is
  followed by coordinate scaling by `w` for BN254 and inverse `w` for BLS12-381.
  For the prefixes `bn254_twist` and `bls12381_twist`, use
  `*_embedding_group_homo`, `*_embedding_inj`, `*_embedding_scalar`,
  `*_embedding_order` and `*_embedded_generator_order`.

These are **injective group homomorphisms**, not surjections onto all
extension-field rational points. Scalar and order preservation require membership
in the source group carrier. The embedded standard generator retains order
`bn254_n` or `bls12381_n`; this does not state the cardinality of the fp12 curve.
The theory supplies embeddings, not a pairing function.

## Reading and using the interfaces

* **Representation.** Prime-field coordinates are natural-number residues.
  Quadratic-field coordinates are normalized ascending-coefficient lists:
  `[real;imaginary]` denotes `real + imaginary*i`, with `i^2 = -1`, and `[]`
  is zero. These are polynomial-quotient fields, not tuple implementations.
  Points are `ECAffine x y` or `ECInfinity`; group `.exp` is scalar multiplication.
* **Full group versus generated subgroup.** A concrete `*_group` is the full
  `sw_group` of rational points over its stated field; `*_subgroup` is
  `Generated` by the standard generator. Generator order and subgroup
  cardinality alone do not determine the full group cardinality.
  `cofactorOneInstances` identifies the two for secp256k1, P-256 and BN254 G1.
  BLS12-381 G1 and both G2 twists have separate full-cardinality results and
  non-fullness/properness results for their standard prime-order subgroups.
* **Full versus effective cofactors.** Here a full cofactor is the full curve
  cardinality divided by the named scalar prime. BLS12-381 G1 uses
  `bls12381_full_cofactor`, while G2 uses `bls12381_g2_full_cofactor`; these
  are different constants for different curves. The G1 factorization is
  `3 * bls12381_torsion_factor ** 2`. The G2 argument uses two cyclic factors,
  including the factor 299, rather than a single full-order generator.
  Do not substitute effective hash-to-curve cofactors into these count formulas;
  these theories do not establish an effective-cofactor hash-to-curve algorithm.
* **Clearing conclusions.** The G2 cofactor-clearing theorems assume curve-point
  membership and conclude membership in the scalar-prime torsion group. They do
  not state membership in the named standard generated subgroup. The BN254
  full-generator clearing theorem additionally gives exact scalar-prime order.
* **Dependencies and trust.** Import the instance theory for generator facts,
  its cardinality theory for full counts, and `pairingTwistMorphisms` for fp12
  embeddings. Named prime fields, polynomial-quotient field certificates and
  generic curve/group results supply the mathematical premises. Literal point
  coordinates, Lucas witnesses and scalar milestones are proposed certificate
  data: the proof scripts use HOL conversions and inference to check their
  claims, not external point-count or primality verdicts. The long certificate
  theories contain computation details; the entry points above avoid local
  arithmetic lemmas.
